### Bounding Box Rules
- **Coordinates**: `box_2d: [ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax]` normalized to `0..1000` (`0 <= ymin < ymax <= 1000`, `0 <= xmin < xmax <= 1000`).
- **Full visual coverage**: Enclose the entire visual block on this page—all sub-panels, drawings, compound/panel labels, reaction arrows/conditions, axes, legends, and table headers, rows, and table footnotes.
- **Exclude external text**: Exclude the main figure/table caption paragraph, surrounding article body text, page headers/footers, and neighboring visuals.
